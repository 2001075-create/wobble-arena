  • Sunny Farm is bigger, with a big barn you can walk into (hay bales inside and stairs up to a covered hayloft). The animals are gone so it is less crowded. It now sits where the Warehouse used to be in the level list

  • Crates drop about twice as often, and more can be on the floor at once
  • The bot slider's yellow bar now follows the circle, and leaving an online room puts the rules back to normal with 3 bots
  • The Shop and Locker now have the live bot battle behind them, and the bots keep fighting behind the menu while you wait in an online room
  • The online room bar now has a Leave room button, and the coins and buttons sit below it instead of under it
  • Level page: the spinning view is a bit higher and closer, with a strip of background showing
  • The city background is redone as a cleaner skyline
Build 75
  • The Shop and the Locker are redone to match the main menu: full screen, a big wobbly title, your wobbler in a card with a sharper preview, and a grid of rarity-coloured item cards with gold Buy and Wear buttons
Build 74
  • New level: Sunny Farm. A big red barn with a hayloft, a chicken coop with chickens and chicks, a tractor, cows, a pig pen, a haystack to climb in the middle, a windmill, a silo, a duck pond, a crop field with a scarecrow, and a farm skyline with barns, windmills and doves
  • The level backgrounds are now much lighter on the graphics card, so they cost far less speed
  • Level backgrounds: trees on every level, a huge detailed castle on a mountain, a new city skyline with a needle tower, domed hall and bridge, many more pink and white cones on Candy Land, a bigger haunted mansion with a dead forest, ghosts and bats, and a deep-space Moonbase with nebulas, a space station, asteroids and a comet

  • The level page preview is now a live, sharp view of the level with the bots fighting, instead of a blurry turntable with frozen bots. Drag it to turn it

Twelve weapons only: the shotgun, Mammoth Pistol, revolver, sniper, rocket, grenade and cluster launchers all shove you around, while the AK, SMG, Uzis, minigun and net gun do not. No melee, flamethrower, snake shooter, air blower, snowball cannon, ninja weapons or bow. Two exclusive maps: Crater Dome (a lunar crater under a glass dome with floating asteroid platforms) and Space Capsule (the inside of a capsule with windows onto space)
  • Online redesigned: Play online is now a clean hub with Quick play, Browse rooms, Create a room and Join with a code, each on its own page, plus a tidy lobby with player seats and settings. The game can now use a real game server for the room list and connections (it falls back to direct connections when there is none)
  • Online rebuilt: no codes needed. Click a mode under Quick Play and you are put in a game with other players, or you start one. Bots fill the empty spots and one steps out every time a player joins.   • Chat for online matches (Enter to type, no word filter, /mute name to hide someone, switch it off in Settings). The map can be switched on and off with N, K.O.s and the board moved to the left, and the Mammoth headshot is now 230 damage

  • Online: Pillars now works online (everyone starts on their own pillar, the host keeps the 3-minute clock, falls and K.O.s count for everybody, and when you are out you spectate). Mega Damage online now really plays as Mega Damage: only its own weapons drop (no more SMGs or bows), double damage, 3 lives and the shrinking arena. The room used to throw away the host's mode and run everything as Classic. Bows are also gone from Mega Damage offline. Falling off the edge now counts as a K.O. for the player who knocked you off, in every online mode
  • Weapons no longer auto-switch when you pick them up (unless your current one is empty). It is a toggle in Settings
  • New HUD: map top right, then the leaderboard, your K.O. score and the recent kills, all stacked down the right. The controls list moved to the pause menu and Settings, and the Ninja dash shows its key
  • Pillars rebuilt: eight pillars in a ring with an empty middle, too far apart to jump (use a dash), a different bit of cover on each pillar, and one life: fall or die and you are out, then spectate or start a new match
